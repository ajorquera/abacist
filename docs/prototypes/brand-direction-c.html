Warm personal

A humanist, earthy identity for a private money notebook. Personality lives on the surfaces; the numbers stay plain, neutral and tabular.

01 — Mark & wordmark

A cairn of counting stones

Abacist
Abacist
Abacist

“Calculus” is Latin for pebble: the first abacists counted with stones. Three stacked stones read as a small cairn — balanced, built up by hand, slightly imperfect. Wordmark is Alegreya Sans Bold, tight tracking, sentence case.
