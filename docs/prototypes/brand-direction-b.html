Abacist · Brand direction B — Calm modern

Quiet, precise, trustworthy. Brand lives on the surfaces (header, wordmark, empty states); numbers, tables and charts stay neutral and use tabular figures.

Wordmark & mark

An open apex — an “A” with no crossbar — sitting over a level line: something rising, measured against a steady baseline. No abacus, no beads.

Palette (light theme tokens)

Cool slate neutrals, one accent. Accent is indigo, not teal: teal sits too close to the gain-green and would blur “brand” with “money went up”. Indigo is unambiguous — it never reads as a gain or a loss.
